Moon in Capricorn

What this placement means in a birth chart, in plain words.

A birth chart is a map of the sky at the moment and place someone was born. Every planet in the chart stands for one part of life. Moon stands for feelings, comfort, and needs. The sign a planet sits in colours how that part of a person tends to work, a bit like the mood it works in.

Below is the reading for Moon in Capricorn. It is the same text that appears inside a chart result when this placement shows up there, word for word. One placement is never the whole person: a full chart holds ten of them, and the houses besides.

With the Moon in Capricorn, you often feel settled when life feels under control. Having a plan, finishing what you promised, and knowing the basics are handled tend to calm you. You may keep feelings private at first and work through them by doing something useful. Rest can feel hard to justify, yet quiet, simple comforts and time in nature often restore you deeply. As you grow more comfortable asking for support, you may find that steady, reliable kindness from others settles you most of all.

Is Moon in Capricorn in your chart?

There is one way to know: draw your own chart. If Moon was in Capricorn when and where you were born, this reading is one of yours, and your chart result will show it alongside the other nine planets.
