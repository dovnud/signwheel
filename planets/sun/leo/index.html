Sun in Leo

What this placement means in a birth chart, in plain words.

A birth chart is a map of the sky at the moment and place someone was born. Every planet in the chart stands for one part of life. Sun stands for identity, vitality, and the wish to be seen. The sign a planet sits in colours how that part of a person tends to work, a bit like the mood it works in.

Below is the reading for Sun in Leo. It is the same text that appears inside a chart result when this placement shows up there, word for word. One placement is never the whole person: a full chart holds ten of them, and the houses besides.

A Leo Sun often describes someone who lights up when they can create, play, and be truly seen. You tend to draw energy from praise that feels honest, from making something you are proud of, and from time with people who enjoy your company. Being ignored or stuck doing dull work in the background can flatten you. You may find your confidence grows when you share what you make. Generosity is often part of the picture too, since cheering others on tends to lift you as well.

Is Sun in Leo in your chart?

There is one way to know: draw your own chart. If Sun was in Leo when and where you were born, this reading is one of yours, and your chart result will show it alongside the other nine planets.
