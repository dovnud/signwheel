Sun in Cancer

What this placement means in a birth chart, in plain words.

A birth chart is a map of the sky at the moment and place someone was born. Every planet in the chart stands for one part of life. Sun stands for identity, vitality, and the wish to be seen. The sign a planet sits in colours how that part of a person tends to work, a bit like the mood it works in.

Below is the reading for Sun in Cancer. It is the same text that appears inside a chart result when this placement shows up there, word for word. One placement is never the whole person: a full chart holds ten of them, and the houses besides.

With the Sun in Cancer, home and the people you trust often give you your energy back. Cancer is linked with care and belonging, and you may find you feel most yourself in familiar places with people who know you well. Time to retreat and recharge in private tends to matter to you. You often lead with kindness and a good memory for what matters to others. When you feel safe and settled, your warmth and loyalty tend to come through most clearly.

Is Sun in Cancer in your chart?

There is one way to know: draw your own chart. If Sun was in Cancer when and where you were born, this reading is one of yours, and your chart result will show it alongside the other nine planets.
