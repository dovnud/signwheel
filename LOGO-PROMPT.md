## Main prompt (concept A: the wheel itself)

The mark: an astrological chart wheel drawn as a fine brass instrument ring, like an engraved plate from an old star atlas. A perfect circle with a thinner inner circle, divided into 12 equal segments by short radial ticks, one small bright four-point star sitting on the rim at upper right, and a tiny solid sun disc at the exact centre. Flat vector logo style, clean precise line work, single gold colour (#d9a441) on a deep night-sky ink background (#0a0e1c). Elegant, calm, crafted, a little playful, NOT mystical or new-age. No gradients, no glow, no purple, no zodiac glyphs, no moons, no crystals, no text, no letters. The mark must stay readable at tiny favicon size, so keep the lines bold enough and the shapes simple. Show the symbol large and centred, with generous empty space around it.
