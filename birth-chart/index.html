The birth chart

Your chart, drawn honestly.

Three facts make a chart: the day you were born, the time if you know it, and the town. Everything else is arithmetic, and we show the arithmetic.

Your birth details stay on your device

We do not ask for an account, a password, or an email address. Your chart is worked out in this browser, and your birth details are not saved on our servers. If you save a chart, it is saved in this browser, on this device, and you can delete it whenever you like. The full detail is in our Privacy Policy.
