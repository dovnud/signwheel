What this site is

Everything on SignWheel, including birth charts, compatibility charts, daily transits, horoscopes, and all written interpretations, is provided for entertainment, education about astrological traditions, and personal reflection.

People have read meaning into chart patterns for a very long time. We present those traditional interpretations in plain language so you can think with them. We do not claim that a chart can predict future events, reveal what another person thinks or will do, or tell you what decision to make, and nothing on this site should be read that way.

Not professional advice

SignWheel is not a medical, mental health, legal, financial, or other professional service, and nothing here is a substitute for one.

If a question is about your health, your mind, or your body, ask a qualified health professional. If a question is about money, investing, debt, or a business decision, ask a qualified financial professional. If a question is about your rights, a contract, or a dispute, ask a lawyer. If you are in crisis or feel unsafe, contact your local emergency services or a crisis line in your country right away. A chart is not help in an emergency.

Decisions stay yours. If a reading and your own judgment point in different directions, trust your judgment, and get a professional opinion where the decision matters.

No guarantees

We do not guarantee that any interpretation will fit your life, feel accurate, or lead to any particular outcome, and we do not sell or offer any service that claims to remove curses, lift blockages, change luck, heal illness, or influence another person. If anyone using our name offers you such a service for payment, it is not us.

Your birth details are calculated and saved on your own device; we never ask for an account or an email, and we hold no copy.
