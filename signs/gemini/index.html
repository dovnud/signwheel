The signs

Gemini

Gemini is one of the three air signs. This page gathers everything SignWheel has written about Gemini so far.

A longer portrait of the sign is still being written. Rather than pad this page, here are the finished pieces: the Sun, Moon and rising readings for Gemini, exactly as they appear inside a chart result, and a doorway to every planet in Gemini.

Sun in Gemini

Your core character and where your energy comes from.

If your Sun is in Gemini, curiosity often sits at the center of who you are. You tend to gain energy from conversation, reading, and learning how things connect. A day with new ideas and friendly talk often leaves you brighter than a quiet day alone. Boredom can be a real drain for you. You may find you understand yourself better by talking things through or writing them down. Variety helps you feel awake, and having more than one interest on the go often suits you well.

Moon in Gemini

Your feelings and what settles you.

If your Moon is in Gemini, talking is often how your feelings sort themselves out. You may find that naming what you feel, texting a friend, or reading about a similar experience helps you settle. Quiet that lasts too long, with no one to share thoughts with, can leave you restless. Your mood tends to shift quickly, and light conversation or a change of scene often lifts it. Having books, music, or something interesting nearby tends to feel like emotional comfort to you.

Rising in Gemini

The face you show first, before people know you.

With Gemini rising, people often meet your curious, chatty side first. You tend to ask questions, share a quick joke, and pick up the thread of any conversation with ease. Others may see you as bright, youthful, and always a little in motion. Restlessness can show on the surface even when you feel settled inside. New people often assume you know a bit about everything, and they may come to you for news, ideas, and introductions long before they know your deeper feelings.

Every planet in Gemini

  • Sun in Gemini identity, vitality, and the wish to be seen
  • Moon in Gemini feelings, comfort, and needs
  • Mercury in Gemini thinking, speaking, and learning
  • Venus in Gemini affection, attraction, taste, and closeness
  • Mars in Gemini drive, desire, temper, and action
  • Jupiter in Gemini growth, confidence, and the search for meaning
  • Saturn in Gemini effort, limits, patience, and experience
  • Uranus in Gemini change, independence, and fresh perspectives
  • Neptune in Gemini imagination, sympathy, and dreams
  • Pluto in Gemini deep change and candor about power
