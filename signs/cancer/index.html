The signs

Cancer

Cancer is one of the three water signs. This page gathers everything SignWheel has written about Cancer so far.

A longer portrait of the sign is still being written. Rather than pad this page, here are the finished pieces: the Sun, Moon and rising readings for Cancer, exactly as they appear inside a chart result, and a doorway to every planet in Cancer.

Sun in Cancer

Your core character and where your energy comes from.

With the Sun in Cancer, home and the people you trust often give you your energy back. Cancer is linked with care and belonging, and you may find you feel most yourself in familiar places with people who know you well. Time to retreat and recharge in private tends to matter to you. You often lead with kindness and a good memory for what matters to others. When you feel safe and settled, your warmth and loyalty tend to come through most clearly.

Moon in Cancer

Your feelings and what settles you.

With the Moon in Cancer, feeling at home is often what settles you most. Cancer is the sign most linked with the Moon, and you may be especially tuned in to the mood at home and to how your loved ones are doing. Familiar food, old photos, and being somewhere you feel fully safe tend to soothe you. You often need privacy when feelings run strong. Once you have had quiet time to feel things through, you tend to return warm, caring, and ready to look after others.

Rising in Cancer

The face you show first, before people know you.

Cancer rising often makes a gentle, caring first impression. You may come across as kind and a little guarded at first, the sort of person who checks how everyone is doing before settling in yourself. People tend to tell you personal things early, sensing you will listen without judging. Your mood can show on your face more than you realize. Once people know you, the guard drops and your humor and protectiveness show. First meetings often leave others feeling looked after and welcome around you.

Every planet in Cancer

Each planet colours Cancer differently. The full set of readings for this sign:

  • Sun in Cancer identity, vitality, and the wish to be seen
  • Moon in Cancer feelings, comfort, and needs
  • Mercury in Cancer thinking, speaking, and learning
  • Venus in Cancer affection, attraction, taste, and closeness
  • Mars in Cancer drive, desire, temper, and action
  • Jupiter in Cancer growth, confidence, and the search for meaning
  • Saturn in Cancer effort, limits, patience, and experience
  • Uranus in Cancer change, independence, and fresh perspectives
  • Neptune in Cancer imagination, sympathy, and dreams
  • Pluto in Cancer deep change and candor about power
