The signs

Pisces

Pisces is one of the three water signs. This page gathers everything SignWheel has written about Pisces so far.

A longer portrait of the sign is still being written. Rather than pad this page, here are the finished pieces: the Sun, Moon and rising readings for Pisces, exactly as they appear inside a chart result, and a doorway to every planet in Pisces.

Sun in Pisces

Your core character and where your energy comes from.

With the Sun in Pisces, gentleness and imagination often sit close to your core. You tend to recharge near water, music, art, and quiet time where your mind can drift. Harsh noise, conflict, and packed schedules often leave you tired in a way sleep alone does not fix. You may find you pick up the mood in a room very quickly. Kindness tends to come naturally to you, and helping someone feel less alone often gives you energy back, as long as you also protect time to rest.

Moon in Pisces

Your feelings and what settles you.

With the Moon in Pisces, your feelings often soak up the room around you. Music, water, sleep, and quiet time alone tend to settle you when the world feels like too much. You may cry easily at kind or sad moments, and that release often helps. Harsh voices and rushed places can leave you unsettled for hours. Gentle routines, time near the sea or in the bath, and being with kind people tend to restore you. Clear, soft boundaries often help your calm last longer.

Rising in Pisces

The face you show first, before people know you.

Pisces rising often makes a soft, dreamy, and kind first impression. You may come across as gentle and easy to approach, with a manner that shifts a little to match the people around you. Others tend to sense your empathy early and may open up to you quickly. You can seem a little far away at times, as if part of your attention lives in music, memory, or imagination. People often remember your kindness first. Later they discover the quiet strength and humor underneath it.
