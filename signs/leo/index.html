The signs

Leo

Leo is one of the three fire signs. This page gathers everything SignWheel has written about Leo so far.

A longer portrait of the sign is still being written. Rather than pad this page, here are the finished pieces: the Sun, Moon and rising readings for Leo, exactly as they appear inside a chart result, and a doorway to every planet in Leo.

Sun in Leo

Your core character and where your energy comes from.

A Leo Sun often describes someone who lights up when they can create, play, and be truly seen. You tend to draw energy from praise that feels honest, from making something you are proud of, and from time with people who enjoy your company. Being ignored or stuck doing dull work in the background can flatten you. You may find your confidence grows when you share what you make. Generosity is often part of the picture too, since cheering others on tends to lift you as well.

Moon in Leo

Your feelings and what settles you.

A Leo Moon often means you settle best when you feel appreciated and warmly included. Kind attention, play, and a chance to share something you made tend to lift your mood. Feeling overlooked can sting more than you may like to admit. You often feel better after fun, laughter, and time with people who enjoy you openly. Creative play is not a luxury for you. It is often one of the main ways your heart recharges and your feelings find their balance again.

Rising in Leo

The face you show first, before people know you.

With Leo rising, you often come across as warm, proud, and hard to miss. Your hair, smile, or the way you take up space in a friendly way tends to get noticed. People may assume you are confident and happy to be the center of attention, even on days you do not feel that way inside. You often greet others with real generosity, and your playful side shows early. Strangers may remember you after one meeting, and friends often look to you to lift the mood.

Every planet in Leo

Each planet colours Leo differently. The full set of readings for this sign:

  • Sun in Leo identity, vitality, and the wish to be seen
  • Moon in Leo feelings, comfort, and needs
  • Mercury in Leo thinking, speaking, and learning
  • Venus in Leo affection, attraction, taste, and closeness
  • Mars in Leo drive, desire, temper, and action
  • Jupiter in Leo growth, confidence, and the search for meaning
  • Saturn in Leo effort, limits, patience, and experience
  • Uranus in Leo change, independence, and fresh perspectives
  • Neptune in Leo imagination, sympathy, and dreams
  • Pluto in Leo deep change and candor about power
