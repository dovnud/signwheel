The signs

Libra

Libra is one of the three air signs. This page gathers everything SignWheel has written about Libra so far.

A longer portrait of the sign is still being written. Rather than pad this page, here are the finished pieces: the Sun, Moon and rising readings for Libra, exactly as they appear inside a chart result, and a doorway to every planet in Libra.

Sun in Libra

Your core character and where your energy comes from.

If your Sun is in Libra, balance and good company often feed you most. Libra is linked with fairness and partnership, and you tend to feel energized around beauty, music, and conversation where everyone gets heard. Being in the middle of conflict or in a harsh, ugly setting can drain you quickly. You may find you know your own mind better after talking an idea over with someone you trust. Time spent making a space pleasant, or smoothing things between people, often leaves you feeling restored.

Moon in Libra

Your feelings and what settles you.

With the Moon in Libra, peace around you often shapes peace inside you. Pleasant rooms, soft music, and kind company tend to settle you, while arguments or unfair treatment can linger in your mood. You may find you process feelings best in conversation, especially with someone who listens well. Making a space look and feel nicer often lifts you more than you expect. Rest tends to come easiest when things feel balanced, and when you know the people close to you are getting along.

Rising in Libra

The face you show first, before people know you.

With Libra rising, charm is often the first thing people notice. You tend to smile easily, listen well, and make conversation feel smooth, even with strangers. Others may see you as fair, stylish, and easy to be around. You often work quietly to keep things pleasant, so people can mistake your calm manner for having no strong views. In time they learn you think carefully about both sides. First impressions of you often include how comfortable you made everyone else feel.

  • Moon in Libra feelings, comfort, and needs
  • Mercury in Libra thinking, speaking, and learning
  • Venus in Libra affection, attraction, taste, and closeness
  • Mars in Libra drive, desire, temper, and action
  • Jupiter in Libra growth, confidence, and the search for meaning
  • Saturn in Libra effort, limits, patience, and experience
  • Uranus in Libra change, independence, and fresh perspectives
  • Neptune in Libra imagination, sympathy, and dreams
  • Pluto in Libra deep change and candor about power
