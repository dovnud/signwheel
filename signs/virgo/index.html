The signs

Virgo

Virgo is one of the three earth signs. This page gathers everything SignWheel has written about Virgo so far.

A longer portrait of the sign is still being written. Rather than pad this page, here are the finished pieces: the Sun, Moon and rising readings for Virgo, exactly as they appear inside a chart result, and a doorway to every planet in Virgo.

Sun in Virgo

Your core character and where your energy comes from.

With the Sun in Virgo, you often feel most energized when you are making something work better. Sorting a mess, learning a skill properly, and seeing small details fall into place tend to satisfy you deeply. You may recharge through quiet routines, tidy spaces, and tasks you can complete well. Feeling useless or surrounded by chaos often wears you down. Many people with this placement find that being helpful in a practical way is not a chore at all. It is often where their sense of purpose comes from.

Moon in Virgo

Your feelings and what settles you.

If your Moon is in Virgo, small order often brings emotional calm. Tidying one drawer, making a list, or fixing a small problem tends to settle your mind when feelings feel messy. You may feel unsettled by clutter, vague plans, or the sense that you have not done enough. Gentle routines around food, rest, and work often help you feel safe. Being useful to someone in a practical way can soothe you too, though learning to rest without earning it first may take practice.

Rising in Virgo

The face you show first, before people know you.

Virgo rising often gives a neat, thoughtful, and capable first impression. You may come across as observant and well prepared, someone who notices the detail everyone else walked past. People tend to ask you practical questions early, sensing you will have a sensible answer. You can seem a little reserved at first, because you often prefer to size up a situation before relaxing into it. As people get to know you, your dry humor and quiet kindness tend to be the things they value most.

Every planet in Virgo

  • Moon in Virgo feelings, comfort, and needs

  • Venus in Virgo affection, attraction, taste, and closeness
  • Mars in Virgo drive, desire, temper, and action
  • Jupiter in Virgo growth, confidence, and the search for meaning
  • Saturn in Virgo effort, limits, patience, and experience
  • Uranus in Virgo change, independence, and fresh perspectives
  • Neptune in Virgo imagination, sympathy, and dreams
  • Pluto in Virgo deep change and candor about power
