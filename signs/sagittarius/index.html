The signs

Sagittarius

Sagittarius is one of the three fire signs. This page gathers everything SignWheel has written about Sagittarius so far.

A longer portrait of the sign is still being written. Rather than pad this page, here are the finished pieces: the Sun, Moon and rising readings for Sagittarius, exactly as they appear inside a chart result, and a doorway to every planet in Sagittarius.

Sun in Sagittarius

Your core character and where your energy comes from.

With the Sun in Sagittarius, you may find that space to explore is what keeps your energy up. Travel, big questions, learning about other ways of life, and plenty of time outdoors tend to suit you. Feeling boxed in by routine or narrow rules often drains you fast. You tend to feel most yourself when you are honest, hopeful, and aiming at something further away. Laughter helps you a lot too. Sharing what you have learned with others often completes the pleasure for you.

Moon in Sagittarius

Your feelings and what settles you.

If your Moon is in Sagittarius, fresh air and a wider view often settle your feelings. A walk, a drive, a funny story, or planning a trip can lift your mood when it dips. Feeling trapped in routine or heavy moods with no outlet tends to make you restless. You often cheer up by remembering the bigger picture and what still feels possible. Time outdoors and honest, good humored company tend to restore you, and you usually feel better after moving than after sitting and brooding.

Rising in Sagittarius

The face you show first, before people know you.

With Sagittarius rising, you often come across as open, upbeat, and easy to talk to. A laugh that arrives quickly and a friendly, direct manner tend to shape how strangers see you. People may assume you love travel, big ideas, and a good story, and they often start sharing theirs. You can seem restless in formal settings, as if part of you is already planning the next outing. Your honesty shows early too, usually delivered with enough good humor that people take it well.
