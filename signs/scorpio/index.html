The signs

Scorpio

Scorpio is one of the three water signs. This page gathers everything SignWheel has written about Scorpio so far.

A longer portrait of the sign is still being written. Rather than pad this page, here are the finished pieces: the Sun, Moon and rising readings for Scorpio, exactly as they appear inside a chart result, and a doorway to every planet in Scorpio.

Sun in Scorpio

Your core character and where your energy comes from.

A Scorpio Sun often belongs to someone who gains energy from depth rather than noise. Honest talks, work that feels meaningful, and mysteries you can dig into tend to draw you in. Small talk and shallow praise may leave you flat, while real trust recharges you. You often prefer to keep your inner life private until someone has earned a closer look. Many people with this placement find they feel strongest when they face something difficult directly and come out the other side changed by what they learned.

Moon in Scorpio

Your feelings and what settles you.

A Scorpio Moon often means your feelings run deep, even when your face stays calm. You may settle best in private, with time to sit with what you really feel instead of explaining it too soon. Trust matters a great deal to you, and you tend to relax only with people who keep confidences and mean what they say. Intense music, honest talks late at night, or time alone to reset often help. Once you feel truly safe, your loyalty and emotional courage tend to show clearly.

Rising in Scorpio

The face you show first, before people know you.

Scorpio rising often makes an intense, private first impression. You may come across as calm and watchful, saying less than you notice, with a gaze people remember. Others can sense there is more going on under the surface, and some may find that intriguing while others find it hard to read. You tend to decide slowly who gets your trust. People often assume you are strong and self contained. Those who know you well learn how loyal and deeply feeling you can be.
