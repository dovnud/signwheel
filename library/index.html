The library

Readings, placement by placement.

Every reading on SignWheel lives here on its own page, free to read with no account. The same texts appear inside your chart result when the placement is yours.

A birth chart is read one placement at a time: a planet in a sign, a planet in a house. This library holds the finished pieces, organised the way a chart is organised. It grows in the open, family by family, and unfinished families say so rather than padding themselves out.
