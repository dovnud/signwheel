Planet in house

Venus in the seventh house

What this placement means in a birth chart, in plain words.

The wheel of a birth chart is cut into twelve sections called houses. Each house stands for one area of life, and the seventh house covers close partners, in love and in work. A planet in a house brings its part of life into that area. Venus stands for affection, attraction, taste, and closeness, so Venus in the seventh house puts that part of a person into the seventh house's ground.

Below is the reading, the same text that appears inside a chart result when this placement shows up there, word for word. Houses need a birth time: without one, a chart has no houses, and this reading does not apply.

Venus is the part of a chart linked with affection, attraction, taste, and closeness. In the seventh house, the area of partnerships and close one-to-one ties, it colours promises, negotiations, and living or working closely. Here, you may seek warmth, agreement, beauty, and mutual ease. The easy side is tact, affection, and a sense of proportion; the rub is smoothing over a difference, seeking approval, or choosing comfort over candor, especially when a partner disagrees or a promise needs revisiting. A practical step is to ask for the other person’s view before defending your first answer, and notice whether the relationship has room for two clear positions.
