Planet in house

Venus in the eighth house

What this placement means in a birth chart, in plain words.

The wheel of a birth chart is cut into twelve sections called houses. Each house stands for one area of life, and the eighth house covers what you share with others: money, trust, and deep change. A planet in a house brings its part of life into that area. Venus stands for affection, attraction, taste, and closeness, so Venus in the eighth house puts that part of a person into the eighth house's ground.

Below is the reading, the same text that appears inside a chart result when this placement shows up there, word for word. Houses need a birth time: without one, a chart has no houses, and this reading does not apply.

Venus is the part of a chart linked with affection, attraction, taste, and closeness. In the eighth house, the area of shared resources, intimacy, and trust, it colours joint plans, private disclosures, and changes neither person controls alone. Here, you may seek warmth, agreement, beauty, and mutual ease. The easy side is tact, affection, and a sense of proportion; the rub is smoothing over a difference, seeking approval, or choosing comfort over candor, especially when trust is strained or a shared arrangement changes. A practical step is to state the shared terms plainly first, and notice whether a difficult subject feels discussable rather than shadowy.
