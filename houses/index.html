Planets in houses

Sixty readings: the Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus and Mars in each of the twelve houses.

A sign says how a planet works; a house says where in life it does its work. House readings need a birth time, because the houses turn with the hours. These are the same texts that appear inside a chart result when the placement shows up there. The twelve houses

  • The first house covers how you come across, and how you begin things
  • The second house covers money, belongings, and what you value
  • The third house covers everyday talk, learning, siblings, and short trips
  • The fourth house covers home, family, and your roots
  • The fifth house covers fun, romance, making things, and children
  • The sixth house covers daily work, routines, and looking after yourself
  • The seventh house covers close partners, in love and in work
  • The eighth house covers what you share with others: money, trust, and deep change
  • The ninth house covers travel, study, beliefs, and the bigger picture
  • The tenth house covers career, reputation, and what you are known for
  • The eleventh house covers friends, groups, and hopes for the future
  • The twelfth house covers rest, privacy, and the parts of life kept out of sight
